# Privacy policy

**Last updated: 13 November 2026**

This policy explains how Leyway handles personal data when you visit our website, create an account,
use the Leyway platform, or do business with us.

Leyway is a platform for running AI agents, automations, workspace computers, files and sites for
people and organizations.

## Summary

- **Who is responsible:** ReJot Nederland B.V. in Amsterdam operates Leyway (section 1).
- **Your content is yours:** we process what you put into Leyway to provide the service to you. We
  do not use it to train or fine-tune AI models, and we do not sell it (sections 1 and 4).
- **What we collect about you:** account details, usage records, technical data and operational
  telemetry, and analytics if you consent (section 2).
- **Who we share it with:** the service providers that run Leyway, such as hosting, AI model,
  transcription and email providers, and services you choose to connect (section 5).
- **Where it goes:** your workspace's content is stored in the region you choose. Your account
  record, AI model requests, email and some other processing are not bound to that region (section
  6).
- **Your rights:** you can access, correct, delete or export your data, and object to how we use it.
  Email leyway-privacy@rejot.dev (sections 8 and 12).

## 1. Who we are

**Controller.** Leyway is operated by **ReJot Nederland B.V.**, registered with the Dutch Chamber of
Commerce (KvK) under number 96241047, with its registered office in Amsterdam, the Netherlands
("Leyway", "we", "us").

**Our parent company.** ReJot Nederland B.V. is a subsidiary of **ReJot, Inc.**, a US holding
company. ReJot, Inc. does not operate Leyway or take part in processing Customer Data.

**Contact.** For anything about this policy or your data, email **leyway-privacy@rejot.dev**.

### Two roles: controller and processor

Our customers, both individual users and organizations, put their own content into Leyway. This
includes agent conversations, files, workspace computers, automations, emails received at Leyway
addresses, data from connected tools, and the sites they publish ("Customer Data"). Customer Data
can contain personal data about our customers and about other people.

- **For Customer Data, we act on our customers' behalf.** Depending on how a customer uses Leyway,
  the customer may be a controller, or a processor acting for someone else. We are accordingly their
  processor or subprocessor, and process Customer Data only to provide Leyway to them.
- **For everything else in this policy, we are the controller.** This covers accounts, sign-in,
  usage records, analytics and telemetry, security, support, our own business communications and our
  website.

**We do not use Customer Data to train or fine-tune AI models, and we do not sell it.** Section 4
explains what our AI model providers may do with it.

If your personal data is in a Leyway customer's workspace, for example because you emailed them,
used a site they publish, or appear in their files, that customer decides how it is used. Contact
them first. We will help them answer your request.

### 2.2 Customer Data we process for customers

Depending on which features a customer uses, Customer Data can include:

- **Agent conversations:** messages, attachments, tool calls and agent output.
- **Voice dictation:** audio recorded while you dictate goes straight from your browser to our
  transcription provider and is not stored as audio. Only the resulting text is kept in the
  conversation.
- **Files and workspace computers:** files you upload or agents create, including audio files you
  ask an agent to transcribe, and the filesystems and commands of workspace computers.
- **Email and messaging:** emails sent to your Leyway inbound address, emails your agents and
  automations send, and messages through connected channels such as Telegram.
- **Connected tools:** data read from or written to tools you connect, such as GitHub, MCP servers,
  APIs and third-party apps.
- **Sites:** the content of sites you publish, which anyone with the address can see if the site is
  public, and only signed-in people with access if it is private; information visitors send to those
  sites, which is stored in your workspace and is not public unless your site shows it; and records
  of recent requests to each site, such as the path requested and what the site logged. The customer
  who publishes a site decides what it collects from visitors and is responsible for telling them.
- **Marketplace listings:** packages you publish, together with your publisher name, are public.

## 4. AI models and other providers that process your content

Leyway runs AI agents. To do this, the content an agent needs (your messages, files and the results
of its tools) is sent to the AI model provider of the model selected, which processes it to generate
a response. Dictation, and audio files you ask an agent to transcribe, are sent to our transcription
provider. Our subprocessor list names these providers.

We use these providers' business services. Under the terms of those services, they may not use what
we send to train their models. They may keep requests for a limited period, mainly to detect misuse.
We choose these providers, their services and their settings, and we hold them to their terms
through our contracts with them.

## 5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

**Service providers (subprocessors).** We use service providers for hosting, AI models,
transcription, email, connections to third-party apps, analytics and telemetry. Each is bound by a
contract that limits use of the data to providing services to us. Our current list, with what each
provider does and where it processes data, is on our [subprocessors page](/legal/subprocessors). We
update that list before we add a new subprocessor.

**Services you connect.** When you connect a service such as GitHub, an MCP server or an external
API, we send data to it and receive data from it because you asked us to. That service handles the
data under its own terms and privacy policy.

**Others in your workspace.** Members of your organization can see content according to their role.
The organization's owners and admins can also act in its members' personal workspaces. An
organization can be managed by another organization, such as an agency that runs Leyway for it. The
managing organization's members can then act in the managed organization with their role there, and
its owners and admins in the managed organization's members' personal workspaces, without being
listed as members.

**Anyone.** Public sites and marketplace listings you publish are visible to anyone. Private sites
are visible only to signed-in people with access. What visitors send to a site is not public unless
the site shows it.

**Other recipients.** Professional advisers (lawyers, accountants, auditors) under confidentiality;
authorities where the law requires it; and a buyer or successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of
assets, who will be bound by this policy.

## 6. Where your data is stored and international transfers

**Storage region.** When you or your organization sets up a workspace, you choose where it is
stored: the **European Union**, the **United States**, or **no preference**. For the EU or US
options, the workspace's databases (its agents, conversations, automations and settings), its files
and its published sites are stored at rest in that region. With no preference, they can be stored in
any region.

**What the storage region does not cover.** Some data is stored or processed outside your chosen
region, even when your workspace is stored in the EU:

- Account records, such as your name, email address, sign-in sessions and organization memberships,
  are kept in one place for all of Leyway, which may be outside your chosen region.
- Workspace computers, and the snapshots that let them resume, can currently run and be kept in a
  data center near you, which may be outside your storage region.
- Our service providers process some data outside your storage region, mainly in the United States:
  AI model requests, email, connections to apps made through our connector provider, and requests
  passing through our hosting provider's global network, with their logs.
- Marketplace listings are public and are not stored in a particular region.

The subprocessor list shows where each provider processes data.

**Safeguards.** When personal data leaves the European Economic Area, we protect the transfer with
an adequacy decision of the European Commission (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for
certified US companies) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, with additional
measures where needed. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erase** your data. To close your account and delete your data, email leyway-privacy@rejot.dev
  from the address on your account. We delete it on the schedule in section 7, apart from records
  the law requires us to keep, such as accounting records.
- **Restrict** our processing in certain cases.
- **Data portability:** receive data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest, and to direct marketing at any time (see
  below).
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing before the withdrawal. You can
  change your analytics choice through the cookie settings link in the app footer.

> **Your right to object.** Where we process your data based on legitimate interest, you can object
> at any time for reasons relating to your situation. We will then stop, unless we have compelling
> legitimate grounds or need the data for legal claims. You can object to direct marketing at any
> time, without giving a reason, and we will stop. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.

To exercise a right, email **leyway-privacy@rejot.dev**. We respond within one month (extendable by
two further months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you) and may need to verify your
identity. We have not appointed a data protection officer; the same address reaches the people
responsible for privacy at Leyway. If your request concerns Customer Data that a Leyway customer
controls, we will pass it to that customer or help them respond.

If you are an organization member, your organization's owners and admins, and those of an
organization that manages it, can act in your personal workspace and control the content in your
organization (section 5). Some requests, such as deleting organization content, may need to go
through them.

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the Netherlands this is the
Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl). You may also contact the authority in
the EU country where you live or work.

**United Kingdom and Switzerland.** If you are in the UK, you have the same rights under the UK GDPR
and can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). If you are in Switzerland,
you have similar rights under the Federal Act on Data Protection and can complain to the Federal
Data Protection and Information Commissioner (edoeb.admin.ch). Transfers from the UK and Switzerland
are protected by the UK Addendum and the Swiss version of the Standard Contractual Clauses, or by an
adequacy decision.

**Canada.** We process personal information of people in Canada with their consent, which may be
express or implied depending on the context, or as otherwise permitted by law. You can withdraw
consent, and ask to access or correct your personal information, by emailing us. You can complain to
the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

**United States.** See section 12.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

We use **strictly necessary** cookies and browser storage to run Leyway: to keep you signed in,
protect your session, and remember choices such as your selected workspace and interface
preferences. These do not need consent.

We use **analytics** (PostHog, hosted in the EU) in your browser only if you consent through our
cookie banner. Analytics records how Leyway is used, including your user ID, email, name and role
once you are signed in. Session recording is off, text on the page is masked, and query strings are
removed from page addresses before anything is sent. You can withdraw consent at any time through
the cookie settings link in the app footer.

Your analytics choice does not affect the operational telemetry our servers record (section 2.1).
That telemetry uses no cookies and is not used for advertising.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not allow third parties to track you across other
websites through Leyway.

**Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.** There is no common standard for how websites should
respond to Do Not Track signals, so we do not respond to them. We do treat a Global Privacy Control
(GPC) signal from your browser as a refusal of analytics cookies. Because we do not sell or share
personal information for advertising, there is nothing else for these signals to switch off.

## 10. Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. These include
encryption in transit, encryption at rest by our hosting provider, separation of each workspace's
data, role-based access within organizations, and logging and monitoring.

Leyway's system administrators cannot open a personal workspace, or an organization they are not a
member of, through their administrator role. They can make one organization the managing
organization of another, which gives the managing organization's members access (section 5). We do
this only at the request of the organizations involved, or when an organization is created through
an app that manages it. The people who run our infrastructure can technically reach stored data.
They look at Customer Data only when you ask us for support, to investigate security incidents or
misuse, or when the law requires it.

No system is fully secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the competent authority
and affected people where the GDPR requires it, and we will inform affected customers without undue
delay.

## 11. Children

Leyway is a service for work. You must be 18 or older to create an account or use Leyway. We do not
knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 as controller. If you believe someone under 18
has an account or has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. Customer workspaces
can contain information about minors; the customer is responsible for that data.

### Your rights

Depending on the state where you live, you may have the right to:

- Know whether we process your personal information, and access it.
- Know the categories of personal information we collected, its sources, our purposes, and the
  categories of third parties we disclosed it to. In some states you can ask for a list of the
  specific third parties.
- Correct inaccurate personal information.
- Delete personal information.
- Get a copy of your personal information in a portable format.
- Opt out of the sale of personal information, sharing for targeted advertising, and profiling that
  produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do none of these.
- Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We will not deny you the service, charge
  you a different price or give you a different level of service because you used them.

**How to exercise them.** Email leyway-privacy@rejot.dev. We respond within 45 days, and may extend
that by a further 45 days where the law allows, in which case we will tell you.

**Verification.** We verify your identity by matching your request with information we already hold,
usually by asking you to confirm from the email address on your account. We use information provided
for verification only for that purpose.

**Authorized agents.** You can let an authorized agent make a request for you. We will ask the agent
for proof that you authorized them, such as a signed permission, and may ask you to verify your
identity directly with us.

**Appeals.** If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision or emailing
leyway-privacy@rejot.dev with "Appeal" in the subject. We will respond in writing within the period
your state's law sets (usually 45 or 60 days) and explain our decision. If we deny your appeal, you
can contact your state attorney general.

**California "Shine the Light".** California residents can ask once a year which personal
information we disclosed to third parties for their own direct marketing in the previous calendar
year. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing.
